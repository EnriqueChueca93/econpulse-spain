Methods and data

Score. Each speech is split into sentences and searched for the phrases below. The score is 100 × (hawkish − dovish) ÷ (hawkish + dovish). A speech needs at least {{ p.min_matches }} matches, and at least 5% of its sentences must mention monetary policy, inflation or interest rates, to be scored; otherwise it has "no stance". The lists count changes and risks, not levels ("higher inflation", not "high inflation"), because speeches use levels to describe the past as often as the outlook. They miss negation and context: "not yet time to cut" counts as a cut. Members' averages give each member equal weight; meeting cycles run from the Saturday after one meeting to the Friday after the next.

Who counts. Speeches by Governing Council members, recognised by the role printed with each speech: the ECB's President, Vice-President and Executive Board, and the governors of the euro-area national central banks (Croatia's from 2023, Bulgaria's from 2026). Deputy governors are not members and are left out. "Current" members are the latest holder of each seat and board members heard from in the last six months.

Sources. Executive Board speeches from the ECB's full-text file of its speeches{% if p.ecb_last %} (to {{ p.ecb_last }}){% endif %}; national governors from the BIS's archive of central bankers' speeches{% if p.bis_archive_last %}, which currently runs to {{ p.bis_archive_last }}{% endif %}, and, after that, from the BIS feed as speeches appear; speeches between the two are added when the BIS updates its archive. The BIS allows non-commercial use; only scores, titles and links are shown here. Interviews and press conferences are not included.
