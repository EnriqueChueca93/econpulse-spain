Global central bank stance

For twelve central banks: what each did, what it said, where it is in its cycle, when it meets next, what bank economists expect and what markets price, and how the world's central banks are leaning.

From Econvitals. This page shows the Global Central Bank Stance Matrix built by Econvitals (econvitals.org, by Jason Furman), which places its own text, charts and estimates in the public domain (CC0 1.0). EconPulse Spain takes its data once a day and lays it out here, with thanks. The underlying data remain their publishers': policy rates from the BIS and the central banks, desk calls from the named banks' research, market odds from the markets named in each row.
