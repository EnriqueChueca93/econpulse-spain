About the data

Average monthly registrations of the general scheme and the self-employed by two-digit activity (CNAE), from the Social Security's yearly workbooks, since 2021. They leave out the special schemes for farm workers and domestic employees, so agriculture and households look smaller here than in the sector figures, and civil servants covered by their own mutual funds, so public administration does too. Seasonal adjustment is Macroscope's (STL on the series since 2021); the ministry adjusts only the four broad sectors.

From January 2026 activities follow the new classification, CNAE-2025. The groups shown keep their boundaries across the change, except for two moves that cross them, which Macroscope links by moving the registrations involved in the series before 2026{% if moves %}: {{ moves | join("; ") }}{% endif %}. Each move is estimated as the receiving division's jump from December 2025 to January 2026, less the same change a year earlier.
