About the data

Monthly exports and imports of goods in euros from Eurostat's Comext database (DS-045409), by chapter of the Harmonised System, summed into product groups. Comext follows the EU's community concept, so its figures differ slightly from those of Spain's customs and the Ministry of Economy: in 2023 Comext counted €391 billion of exports and €435 billion of imports, a little more than the national figures on both sides. Trade by partner uses the same source, by country of destination for exports and of origin for imports. Not seasonally adjusted; the latest months are provisional.

Services by type come from Eurostat's quarterly balance of payments (BPM6); tourism and travel is what visitors spend in Spain and what Spaniards spend abroad. Export and import prices of industrial goods come from Eurostat's short-term business statistics (non-domestic producer prices and import prices, which INE compiles for Spain), 2021 = 100, by main industrial grouping. The terms of trade in the chart divide the national accounts' export deflator by the import deflator, for goods and services and for goods alone.
