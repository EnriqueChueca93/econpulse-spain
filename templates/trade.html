About the data

Monthly exports and imports of goods in euros from Eurostat's Comext database (DS-045409), by chapter of the Harmonised System, summed into product groups. Comext follows the EU's community concept, so its figures differ slightly from those of Spain's customs and the Ministry of Economy: in 2023 Comext counted €391 billion of exports and €435 billion of imports, a little more than the national figures on both sides. Services, tourism and the current account are in the balance of payments, on the Activity page. Not seasonally adjusted; the latest months are provisional.
